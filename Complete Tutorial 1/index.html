How are the scores calculated?

Words are separated by whitespace. Similarity is twice the number of matching words in sequence divided by the combined comparable word count. Reordered words can count as differences.

Accuracy counts the minimum word insertions, deletions, and replacements. Each score is 100 × (1 − edits ÷ reference words), with a minimum of 0%. A uses Text A as the reference; B uses Text B. Two empty comparable texts score 100%; only one empty scores 0%.

Ignore options affect comparison scores, not the original word counts. Ignored punctuation remains visible; punctuation-only words appear unhighlighted when punctuation is ignored. Different words have a dotted underline as well as a red highlight.
