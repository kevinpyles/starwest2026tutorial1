How are the scores calculated?

Words are whitespace-separated tokens. Similarity = 100 × twice the number of matching words in sequence ÷ total comparable words. Matching uses a longest common subsequence.

Accuracy = 100 × max(0, 1 − word edit distance ÷ reference word count). A and B each serve as their own reference. Insertions, deletions, and substitutions cost one edit each. Two empty comparable texts score 100%; only one empty scores 0% for all percentages.

Ignore options affect matching and scores, not original word counts. Ignored punctuation remains visible; punctuation-only tokens are neutral when ignored.

07A world with a little spark.

ORIGINAL VECTOR ART / CRISP AT EVERY SIZE

Product illustration

Simple paper shapes, tilted with purpose. Use for onboarding and empty states. Keep meaning legible at small sizes.

Armor, abstracted

Angular red planes, warm metallic edges, one cyan highlight. Use as a banner or cover; keep busy art away from reading surfaces.

Our visual language nods to high-tech armor through color and geometry. The mark and illustrations are original; use these assets consistently instead of character portraits or movie stills. Inline SVGs can be saved and reused independently.

Use an 8px spacing rhythm with 4px for fine adjustments. Controls use 8px corners; cards use 16px. Reserve clipped corners for the brand mark.

Wide screens: paired editors. Small screens: one column, A before B. Keep primary controls at least 44px tall.

Readable by design

  • Target 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and essential interface boundaries. Verify new color pairings.
  • Always label fields. Never communicate differences through color alone.
  • Keep cyan keyboard focus visible. Respect reduced motion and browser zoom.
  • Keep text local. Render user content as text, never HTML.

Theme contract
